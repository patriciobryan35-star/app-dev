# Favorite Series: Loki

**Loki** The story centers on Loki Laufeyson, the God of Mischief, who escapes with the Tesseract during the events of *Avengers: Endgame* and creates a branched timeline[cite: 5]. He is immediately apprehended by the Time Variance Authority (TVA)—a bureaucratic organization existing outside of time and space that monitors the Sacred Timeline[cite: 5]. Given a choice between being erased from existence or helping fix the timeline he broke, Loki teams up with TVA agent Mobius to hunt down a dangerous rogue variant, eventually discovering the truth behind the multiverse and his own glorious purpose[cite: 5].

## Why It Stands Out

* **The TVA & Time-Slipping:** Complex time-travel rules, branching timelines, and high-stakes multiverse consequences where every choice can spark a reality-ending war[cite: 5].

## Main Character Highlights

1. **Loki Laufeyson** – The protagonist—a resourceful, silver-tongued trickster who evolves from a self-serving villain into the selfless guardian holding the entire multiverse together[cite: 5].
2. **Mobius M. Mobius** – A dedicated TVA analyst and Loki's closest friend[cite: 5]. Patient, perceptive, and fascinated by Earth's 1990s culture, he is the first person to truly believe Loki can change for the better[cite: 5].
3. **Sylvie** – A fierce, independent Loki variant who spent her life on the run from the TVA[cite: 5]. Driven by vengeance against those who stole her childhood, her actions directly shatter the Sacred Timeline[cite: 5].

# Favorite Movie: Spider-Man: Brand New Day

**Spider-Man: Brand New Day** Following Doctor Strange's spell that erased Peter Parker from everyone's memory, Peter starts over completely alone in a small New York apartment[cite: 5]. Without Stark tech or his closest friends by his side, he returns to his street-level roots—balancing college life, part-time jobs, and protecting neighborhood citizens as Spider-Man while facing new threats emerging across the city[cite: 5].

## Why It Stands Out

* **Street-Level Realism:** Strips away cosmic gadgets to focus on classic, grounded Spider-Man struggles—finances, loneliness, and the heavy personal cost of doing the right thing[cite: 5].

## Main Character Highlights

1. **Peter Parker / Spider-Man** – The protagonist—a resilient, compassionate hero learning how to stand on his own two feet while keeping his promise to protect New York at all costs[cite: 5].
2. **Michelle "MJ" Jones-Watson** – Peter's former girlfriend who no longer remembers their past, yet still crosses paths with him as she pursues her own future[cite: 5].
3. **Ned Leeds** – Peter's loyal best friend who has moved on to college without any memory of being Spider-Man's "guy in the chair"[cite: 5].
